Which travel services does Marjan Travel & Tours provide?

{{icon plus}}

Marjan Travel & Tours provides visa consultancy and processing, Umrah packages, domestic tour packages within Pakistan, international tour packages and corporate tour packages. Air tickets, hotel bookings, travel insurance and airport transfers are arranged as part of these packages or on their own.

Can I book only one part of a trip, such as just the visa or just the hotel?

{{icon plus}}

Yes. You can book a complete package or a single service. Many clients use us only for visa processing, while others book a full package with visa, flights, hotels and tours. We quote each part separately so you can choose.

Do you customise packages or only sell fixed packages?

{{icon plus}}

Both. We run fixed-date group departures for popular trips, such as Umrah, Hunza, Skardu, Dubai and Turkey, and we also build private, fully customised itineraries around your dates, budget and hotel preferences.

Do you serve customers outside Lahore?

{{icon plus}}

Yes. We work with travellers across Pakistan. Most of the process, from the consultation to document checks and payments, can be done on WhatsApp and by bank transfer, and flights can leave from Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, Multan, Peshawar, Faisalabad or Sialkot, depending on availability.

How quickly will I receive a quotation?

{{icon plus}}

Most quotations are sent the same day. Complex corporate or multi-country trips may take a little longer, and we tell you the expected time when you get in touch.
