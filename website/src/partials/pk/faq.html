What is the best time to visit Hunza and Skardu?

{{icon plus}}

The best time to visit Hunza and Skardu is from April to October. April brings cherry and apricot blossom, June to August is peak season when every road and pass is open (including Deosai and Khunjerab), and late September to early November brings golden autumn colours and clear skies.

How many days are enough for a Hunza tour?

{{icon plus}}

From Islamabad, 5 to 7 days is enough for a comfortable Hunza tour by road, covering Karimabad, Attabad Lake, Passu and the Khunjerab Pass. From Lahore or Karachi, add one to two days, or fly to Gilgit to save time. A combined Hunza and Skardu tour needs 8 to 10 days.

Is it better to travel to Skardu by road or by air?

{{icon plus}}

Flying from Islamabad to Skardu takes about an hour and saves at least two days, but flights depend on the weather and can be delayed or cancelled. By road it takes two days each way along the scenic Karakoram Highway, and it is more reliable. Many travellers fly one way and drive the other.

What decides the cost of a northern areas tour?

{{icon plus}}

The destination, the number of days, the hotel category, the vehicle and the size of your group; larger groups pay less per person. Every quotation lists transport, hotels, meals and the guide separately, so you can see exactly what you pay for.

Is it safe to travel to the northern areas of Pakistan?

{{icon plus}}

Yes. The main tourist routes to Hunza, Skardu, Swat, Naran and Murree are travelled by families every season. The main risks are weather, landslides and road conditions, especially in the July–August monsoon. We use experienced local drivers, build buffer days into longer trips and follow official weather and road advisories.

Do you offer Pakistan tour packages from Karachi and Lahore?

{{icon plus}}

Yes. Travellers from Karachi usually fly to Islamabad, Gilgit or Skardu and join the tour there, which saves two days on the road. From Lahore we offer both road departures via the motorway and fly-and-tour options.

Do you arrange honeymoon packages in Pakistan?

{{icon plus}}

Yes. Our honeymoon packages include a private car with driver, hotels with valley or lake views, flexible days and special touches such as room decoration and a private dinner. Hunza, Skardu and Swat are the most popular honeymoon destinations.

What are the best places to visit in Pakistan?

{{icon plus}}

The most popular places to visit in Pakistan are Hunza Valley, Skardu, Fairy Meadows, Swat Valley, Naran & Kaghan, Neelum Valley, Murree and the Galiyat, Lahore’s Walled City and Badshahi Mosque, Islamabad’s Faisal Mosque, and the Khunjerab Pass on the China border. Pakistan is also home to five of the world’s fourteen 8,000-metre peaks, including K2.
