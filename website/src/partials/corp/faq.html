What size of group can you handle for a corporate tour?

{{icon plus}}

We plan corporate trips for groups of every size, from a small leadership team to a large dealer or staff group. Smaller teams can book a private tour with the same level of service, and larger groups get group rates on hotels, transport and flights.

How far in advance should a company book a team trip?

{{icon plus}}

For trips within Pakistan, book 4 to 8 weeks ahead so we can secure enough rooms and vehicles, especially in summer. For international incentive trips, allow 8 to 12 weeks for group visas and flights.

Do you provide invoices suitable for company accounts?

{{icon plus}}

Yes. You receive an itemised quotation, a formal invoice in your company’s name and a final statement after the trip, so your finance team has everything it needs.

Can you organise team-building activities as well as travel?

{{icon plus}}

Yes. Along with transport, hotels and meals, we arrange team-building activities such as guided hikes, river rafting in season, bonfire evenings, treasure hunts and facilitated workshops, matched to your team’s goals and fitness level.

Do you arrange group Umrah for company employees?

{{icon plus}}

Yes. Many companies reward employees or dealers with Umrah. We handle the visas, flights, hotels, transport and guidance for the whole group, with one invoice to the company.

What decides the cost of a corporate trip?

{{icon plus}}

The destination, the group size, the hotel category and the activities you choose. We send a written proposal with two or three options at different budgets, with clear per-person costs, so you can choose what fits.

Can you manage our employees’ regular business travel?

{{icon plus}}

Yes. We can manage flights, hotels and visas for your staff under a corporate account, with a dedicated contact, quick changes and a monthly statement for your finance team.
