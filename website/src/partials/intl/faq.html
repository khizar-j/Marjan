Which is the most affordable international tour from Pakistan?

{{icon plus}}

Dubai, Baku (Azerbaijan), Thailand and Malaysia are usually the most affordable international holidays from Pakistan, thanks to short flights, quick visa processes and good-value hotels. Tell us your dates and budget and we will send a written quotation for each.

What decides the price of an international tour package?

{{icon plus}}

The season, the number of nights, the hotel category, your departure city and the tours you add, such as a hot-air balloon flight in Cappadocia or a desert safari in Dubai. Every quotation lists each part separately, so you can see exactly what you pay for.

Do your international tour packages include the visa?

{{icon plus}}

Yes. Visa assistance is part of every international package: we prepare your documents, submit the application and track it. Whether the official visa fee is included or charged separately is shown clearly on your quotation, as it differs by country.

How far in advance should I book an international holiday?

{{icon plus}}

Book at least 4 to 6 weeks before travel for most destinations, to allow time for the visa. For Europe, and for peak periods such as Eid, December and the summer school holidays, book 2 to 3 months ahead.

Do you offer honeymoon packages from Pakistan?

{{icon plus}}

Yes. Our most popular honeymoon packages from Pakistan are the Maldives, Bali, Thailand (Phuket and Krabi), Turkey (Istanbul and Cappadocia), Malaysia (Langkawi) and Baku. Each includes private transfers, hotels chosen for couples and special touches on request.

Is halal food easy to find on these trips?

{{icon plus}}

Yes. Halal food is widely available in Dubai, Turkey, Azerbaijan, Malaysia and Indonesia, and easy to find in the main tourist areas of Thailand, Singapore, Bali and the Maldives resorts. We add halal restaurants to your itinerary and can arrange halal meal plans.

Can I customise an international tour package?

{{icon plus}}

Yes. Every package can be adjusted: travel dates, number of nights, hotel category, flights from your nearest city and optional activities. You can also combine destinations, for example Malaysia with Singapore, or Umrah with a few days in Dubai or Turkey.
