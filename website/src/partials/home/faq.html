Can a travel agent guarantee my visa?

{{icon plus}}

No. No travel agent or consultant can guarantee a visa, because the final decision always belongs to the embassy. What we can do is prepare a complete, accurate application, flag weak points before you apply, and book the right appointment, which gives you the best possible chance of approval.
