How many days are enough for Umrah?

{{icon plus}}

The Umrah rituals themselves take about 3 to 6 hours. Most Pakistani pilgrims choose 14 to 21 days so they can spend time praying in both Makkah and Madinah without rushing. A 7 to 10-day package works well if you have limited leave, and 28-day packages suit retired pilgrims who want an extended stay.

When is the best time to perform Umrah?

{{icon plus}}

Winter, from November to February, has the most comfortable weather in Makkah and Madinah and is popular with families during the December holidays. Ramadan brings the greatest reward, especially in the last ten nights, but also the highest prices and the largest crowds, so book early. Summer costs less and is quieter, though daytime temperatures often pass 40°C. Umrah visas are paused in the weeks before and during Hajj, so plan your dates around it.

What documents do I need for Umrah from Pakistan?

{{icon plus}}

You need a passport valid for at least 6 months, a CNIC copy, a recent photo with a white background and a polio vaccination certificate for departure from Pakistan. Children need a B-Form or NADRA Family Registration Certificate. We confirm the current health and vaccination requirements, including meningitis, when you book.

Can women perform Umrah without a mahram?

{{icon plus}}

Saudi Arabia no longer requires women to travel with a mahram for Umrah, and many women now travel with organised groups. We arrange women-friendly groups and room sharing. Families should still check any airline rules for minors and young travellers before booking.

Can I perform Umrah on a Saudi tourist visa?

{{icon plus}}

Yes, Saudi tourist e-visa holders are allowed to perform Umrah. However, Pakistani passport holders are generally only eligible for the tourist e-visa if they also hold certain valid visas or residence permits, for example from the USA, the UK or a Schengen country. Most Pakistani pilgrims therefore travel on an Umrah visa arranged by a verified operator.
