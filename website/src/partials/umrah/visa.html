Umrah Visa from Pakistan: 2026–27 Rules

Umrah rules changed in 2025 and 2026. Here is what Pakistani pilgrims need to know before they book.

{{icon plane}}
  1. {{icon shield}} Before you book

    Book with a verified operator

    Pakistani pilgrims must book through an Umrah company verified by the Ministry of Religious Affairs.

    {{icon search-line}}Check the official list
  2. {{icon hotel}} Before the visa

    Hotels and transport come first

    Saudi Arabia issues the Umrah visa only after your hotel and transport are confirmed on Nusuk.

    {{icon check-line}}Confirmed on Nusuk
  3. {{icon calendar}} After the visa is issued

    Enter within 30 days

    Under the current rules, you must enter Saudi Arabia within 30 days of the visa being issued.

    {{icon clock-line}}30 days from issue
  4. {{icon passport}} During your stay

    Leave by the date on your visa

    Your permitted stay is printed on the visa, and Saudi Arabia enforces the exit date strictly.

    {{icon passport-line}}Exit date enforced

    Book your Rawdah slot

    Praying in the Rawdah at Masjid an-Nabawi needs a slot on the Nusuk app. We help you book it.
