What Is Included in Every Umrah Package

One package, one team: from your visa to your Ziyarat, everything below is arranged and confirmed before you fly.

  • {{icon passport}}

    Umrah visa

    Processed through Nusuk, with the Saudi health insurance that comes with it.

  • {{icon plane}}

    Return flights

    Economy flights from your city to Jeddah or Madinah and back.

  • {{icon kaaba}}

    Hotel in Makkah

    For the nights in your package, at the level you choose.

  • {{icon hotel}}

    Hotel in Madinah

    For the nights in your package, near Masjid an-Nabawi.

  • {{icon bus}}

    Transport

    Airport transfers, and Makkah to Madinah by air-conditioned coach.

  • {{icon compass}}

    Ziyarat tours

    Guided visits to the historic sites of Makkah and Madinah.

  • {{icon users}}

    Guidance in Urdu

    A session before you fly on Ihram, Tawaf, Sa'i and travel tips.

  • {{icon calendar}}

    Rawdah booking

    Help booking your Rawdah visit in Masjid an-Nabawi on the Nusuk app.

Upgrades: a private car instead of the coach, or the Haramain high-speed train between the holy cities.

Not included unless stated: meals, the Haramain train, Dam or Qurbani, excess baggage and personal expenses.
