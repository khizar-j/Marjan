What are your office hours?

{{icon plus}}

Our office in Bahria Town, Lahore is open Monday to Saturday, from 9am to 9pm (closed on Sunday), and WhatsApp messages are answered during the same hours.

Do I need an appointment to visit your office?

{{icon plus}}

Walk-ins are welcome during office hours. For visa consultations, we recommend booking a time on WhatsApp first, so a senior consultant is free to review your documents.

Can I book a package without visiting the office?

{{icon plus}}

Yes. Most clients book entirely online: we share the quotation on WhatsApp or by email, you send documents digitally, and payment is made by bank transfer to our official company account. Original passports are only needed when an embassy requires them.

How do I pay, and how do I know a payment request is genuine?

{{icon plus}}

Payments are made only to our official company account in the name of Marjan Travel & Tours, shown on your written quotation; we will never ask you to pay into a personal account. If anyone asks you for payment in our name, call us on +92 303 8244423 to confirm before paying.

How quickly will you reply to my enquiry?

{{icon plus}}

We usually reply to WhatsApp messages and form enquiries the same day, during office hours. Detailed quotations for complex trips may take a little longer.

Do you have offices in other cities?

{{icon plus}}

Our office is in Bahria Town, Lahore. We serve clients across Pakistan by phone, WhatsApp and email, so you can book from any city.
