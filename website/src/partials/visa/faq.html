Can a visa consultant guarantee my visa?

{{icon plus}}

No. Only the embassy or immigration authority can approve a visa, so any agent who “guarantees” a visa is not being honest. What a good consultant does is make sure your application is complete, consistent and well presented, which gives you the best possible chance.

How long does visa processing take?

{{icon plus}}

It depends on the country. A UK Standard Visitor visa is usually decided within about 3 weeks after biometrics, a Schengen visa usually within 15 calendar days of the appointment (longer in peak season), and most e-visas within a few working days. We give you an expected timeline for your visa at the consultation.

How much bank balance do I need for a visit visa?

{{icon plus}}

There is no single fixed amount. Embassies check that your funds comfortably cover the trip and match your declared income, and they look at the last 6 months of transactions. A steady account history usually matters more than one large balance; a sudden large deposit just before applying can raise doubts.

Which countries can Pakistani passport holders visit without a visa?

{{icon plus}}

Only a small number of destinations let Pakistani passport holders travel without a visa or with a visa on arrival. The Maldives, for example, issues a free 30-day visa on arrival to visitors who meet its entry conditions. Because these lists change, contact us for the current options before you book flights.
