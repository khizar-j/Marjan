We Take the Workload Off Your Desk

Marjan Travel & Tours plans corporate tour packages for companies in Pakistan, including annual company trips, team-building retreats, incentive tours abroad, conferences and events, dealer and distributor trips, group Umrah for employees and everyday business travel. Every project has one coordinator, a written proposal and one consolidated invoice.

When HR or admin teams organise a trip for 40 people, the details multiply: rooming lists, vehicles, meals, dietary needs, activities, permissions and budgets. We handle all of it and deliver a well-run trip your team will remember, on time and on budget.
