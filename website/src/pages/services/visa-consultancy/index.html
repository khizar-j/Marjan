Apply Once, and Apply Properly

Marjan Travel & Tours provides visa consultancy and processing in Lahore for Pakistani passport holders. We handle visit, tourist, family, business and Umrah visas for countries including the UAE (Dubai), Saudi Arabia, the UK, Schengen countries, the USA, Canada, Turkey, Azerbaijan, Thailand and Malaysia. The final decision is always made by the embassy.

A visa refusal stays on your travel record, and every future application will ask about it. That is why the way your file is prepared matters as much as the documents themselves. We check every page for gaps and inconsistencies before it reaches the embassy.

Why Visa Applications from Pakistan Get Refused

Most refusals we see come down to five avoidable problems. Our file review is built to catch them before you apply.

Weak or unclear finances

{{icon close}}What the visa officer sees

A balance that does not match your income, or a large deposit made just before applying.

{{icon check}}How we fix it before you apply
  • {{icon check}}We read your 6-month statement the way an officer does.
  • {{icon check}}We match your balance to your salary slips, tax returns or business income.
  • {{icon check}}We explain any large deposit with a document that shows where it came from.

Unclear purpose of travel

{{icon close}}What the visa officer sees

No itinerary, or bookings that do not match the dates on the form.

{{icon check}}How we fix it before you apply
  • {{icon check}}We write a clear day-by-day itinerary for your trip.
  • {{icon check}}We line up your flight and hotel reservations with the dates on your form.
  • {{icon check}}We add a short cover letter that explains why you are travelling.

Weak ties to Pakistan

{{icon close}}What the visa officer sees

Not enough evidence of a job, business, family or property to come back to.

{{icon check}}How we fix it before you apply
  • {{icon check}}We show your job with an NOC or leave letter and recent salary slips.
  • {{icon check}}We add business registration, property or family documents where you have them.
  • {{icon check}}We point out what is missing early, so you have time to arrange it.

Inconsistent documents

{{icon close}}What the visa officer sees

Different spellings of your name, or different dates, across your passport, CNIC, bank and employment letters.

{{icon check}}How we fix it before you apply
  • {{icon check}}We check every name, date and number across all your documents.
  • {{icon check}}We tell you exactly which letter needs correcting, before you apply.
  • {{icon check}}We fill in your form to match your passport exactly.

Undeclared refusals

{{icon close}}What the visa officer sees

Hiding an earlier refusal is treated as deception and can lead to a ban.

{{icon check}}How we fix it before you apply
  • {{icon check}}We declare every earlier refusal, honestly and clearly.
  • {{icon check}}We read the refusal letter and fix the reason it gives.
  • {{icon check}}We tell you when it makes sense to apply again.
