Pakistan’s Mountains, Without the Stress

Marjan Travel & Tours offers domestic tour packages across Pakistan, including Hunza, Skardu, Swat, Naran Kaghan, Fairy Meadows, Murree, Neelum Valley and Kashmir. Tours last 2 to 10 days, leave from Islamabad, Lahore and Karachi by road or by air, and include transport, hotels, breakfast and an experienced guide.

Pakistan is home to K2 and five of the world’s fourteen 8,000-metre peaks, the Karakoram Highway, turquoise glacial lakes and valleys that bloom every spring. We plan the trip so you can simply enjoy it: safe vehicles, drivers who know the mountain roads, clean hotels and a sensible pace for families.

Practical Tips for the Northern Areas

Five things we tell every traveller before a trip to the mountains.

{{icon plane}}
  1. 1

    Plan buffer days

    Mountain roads can close for hours or days after landslides or heavy rain, especially in the monsoon.

  2. 2

    Respect the altitude

    Khunjerab and Babusar are above 4,000 metres. Move slowly, drink plenty of water and rest on arrival.

  3. 3

    Carry cash

    ATMs are few and far between in the remote valleys.

  4. 4

    Expect patchy signal

    Coverage is limited in some valleys; SCOM and a few other networks work in Gilgit-Baltistan.

  5. 5

    Respect local customs

    Dress modestly, and ask before photographing people, especially women.
