Our Story

Marjan Travel & Tours is a licensed travel company in Bahria Town, Lahore. For more than 6 years we have helped Pakistani travellers with Umrah, visas, tours across Pakistan, holidays abroad and company trips, all planned by one team.

Travel from Pakistan should feel simple and honest. Too often, travellers are passed between one agent for the visa, another for the flights and a third for the hotels, and the price changes along the way.

We do it differently. You speak to one consultant, receive one written quotation that lists every cost, and have one number to call from your first question until you are home again. More than 5,500 travellers, including over 2,200 pilgrims, have trusted us with their journeys, and many come back to plan the next one.

The Meaning of Our Name

Marjan is the Arabic and Urdu word for coral. In Surah Ar-Rahman, coral (al-marjan) is named beside pearls as one of the treasures that come out of the seas, among the blessings of Allah.

Coral is precious, grows slowly and lasts. That is how we want every journey with us to feel: planned with care, and remembered for years.

Travelling Responsibly

The valleys, lakes and holy cities we visit should stay beautiful and welcoming for the next traveller, so we plan every trip with the places and the people who live there in mind.

  • {{icon check}}Local people first: we work with local drivers, guides and hotels, so your trip supports the communities you visit.
  • {{icon check}}Leave no trace: we ask every group to carry its litter back from lakes, meadows and camps.
  • {{icon check}}Respect local customs: our guides brief you on dress, photography and prayer times before you arrive.
  • {{icon check}}Safety before sightseeing: we follow official weather and road advisories and change plans when we need to.
